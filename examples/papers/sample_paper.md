# Sample Clinical Biomarker Study (Offline Fixture)

## Section 1: Background
Elevated serum troponin I (> 0.04 ng/mL) is a sensitive and specific biomarker of acute myocardial injury.

## Section 2: Cohort Findings
In a cohort of 240 emergency department patients presenting with chest pain, serial high-sensitivity troponin testing at 0 hours and 1 hour achieved a negative predictive value of 99.6% for ruling out acute myocardial infarction.

## Section 3: Conclusion
A 0/1-hour high-sensitivity troponin protocol safely accelerates triage without compromising diagnostic sensitivity.
